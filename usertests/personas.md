# Personas

One reader per heading. Each is told only its own section.

## maya

**Maya, new birder, phone**

34, a nurse in Brooklyn. Started birding six months ago with the Merlin app and a feeder. Reads on
her phone on the subway, in short bursts, and gives up on a site that makes her work. Knows common
backyard birds by name; does not know what a "wing chord" is. Device: phone.

## dan

**Dan, experienced field birder, laptop**

58, retired engineer in Ohio. Has birded for thirty years, keeps an eBird life list of 600
species, carries a Sibley guide. Skeptical of any number he cannot trace to a source. Patient with
detail if it pays off, impatient with clutter. Device: laptop.

## funmi

**Dr. Funmi Okafor, ornithologist, laptop**

45, studies avian morphology at a university museum. Knows measurement conventions (culmen, wing
chord, mass) and how often published body lengths are missing or estimated. Judges a tool by how
honest it is about what is measured and what is modeled, and whether she could cite it. Device:
laptop.

## lena

**Lena, birder from Germany, phone**

27, a graduate student from Leipzig, visiting the US for a year. Birds in Europe for ten years;
knows birds by their IOC English names (Little Auk, Rough-legged Buzzard) and German ones, and
thinks in centimeters and grams. English is her second language. Device: phone.
