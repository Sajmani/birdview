# Tasks

Every reader gets the same tasks, in this order. For each, give your answer, a confidence from 1
(guess) to 5 (certain), and where on the site you found it.

- **T1. First look.** Look only at the front page, as it first appears on your device. In one or
  two sentences: what is this site for, and what would you do first?
- **T2. Merlin and Blue Jay.** A friend says a Merlin is about the size of a Blue Jay. Is a Merlin
  bigger or smaller, and by how much: length, wingspan, and weight?
- **T3. New York in October.** You are in New York in October. Name three of the birds people
  record most often there at that time of year.
- **T4. Three ducks.** Look at a Mallard, a Wood Duck, and a Canvasback side by side. Which has the
  widest wingspan, and which is the heaviest?
- **T5. Solid and dashed.** In the drawings some lines are solid and some are dashed. What is the
  difference?
- **T6. Sharp-shinned Hawk.** How much does a Sharp-shinned Hawk weigh?

Then, in your own voice:

- **E1.** What confused or annoyed you, if anything?
- **E2.** What, if anything, did you like?
- **E3.** One change you would make.
- **E4.** How easy was the site to use, from 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy)? Would you come back?
