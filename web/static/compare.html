{{/* Once for the page, not once per bird: it describes a convention every panel shares, and repeating it would crowd out what it explains. */}}

Solid lines in the diagram are measured; dashed lines are estimated. The head is hollow because it is estimated too. On the wing, the solid outer ends are the measured wing chord and the ticks mark where it reaches — only the span between them is estimated, and it is dashed when it is.

Each bird links to its own page, where the source or model behind every number is named. The neck/body division is a drawing convention, not a measurement: no source records avian neck length at this coverage.
